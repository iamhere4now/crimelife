Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Naija Detective (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is run by Bamdalas, who is the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy explains what we collect when you play, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Short version: we collect only what the game needs. Other players only see your detective name, look and scores. We never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time in Profile › Account › Delete my account.

2. Our legal reasons (lawful basis)

  • Contract: running your account and the game you signed up for, and processing poster payments.
  • Consent: reminders, optional AI questions and occasional emails about the game. You can withdraw consent at any time (turn reminders off in Profile › App & data; ask us to stop emails).
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the game safe and fair (moderation, bans, rate limits) and fixing problems.
  • Legal obligation: keeping payment records for tax and accounting.

3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run the game for us, and only what they need:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, database, file storage and security.
  • Paystack Payments Ltd: poster payments.
  • Anthropic PBC: answers to Senior Inspector questions, when that feature is on. Don’t put personal details in your questions.
  • Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft: delivery of reminders, through your own browser’s push service.

We don’t sell or rent your personal data. We may disclose it if the law requires it, or to protect players from fraud or harm.

4. Data outside Nigeria

Some of these providers store or process data on servers outside Nigeria. We use reputable providers that protect data with security measures and contractual safeguards, as the NDPA requires for such transfers.

5. How long we keep it

  • Your account and game data: until you delete your account. We may also delete accounts that haven’t been used for 24 months.
  • Chat messages: only your most recent messages are kept; older ones roll off.
  • Problem reports: up to 12 months.
  • Payment records: 6 years, as tax law requires. They stay even if you delete your account, but without your game data.

6. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, get a copy to take elsewhere, and withdraw consent. You can delete your account yourself in Profile › Account › Delete my account. For anything else, contact us (below). We will reply within 30 days. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

7. Children

The game tells non-graphic detective stories. You must be at least 13 to play with an account. If you are under 18, you need your parent or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child has given us data without that permission, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, limits on sign-in attempts and strict access controls. No system is perfectly secure: use a password you don’t use anywhere else. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.

10. Contact

Data controller: Bamdalas, Naija Detective. Contact: use Profile › Report a problem in the game.
